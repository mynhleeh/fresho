# Seed photo credits

All photos below come from Wikimedia Commons. License, author and source page were read from the Commons API (`extmetadata`) on the retrieval date. Each file was downloaded, resized to at most 1600 px on the long edge and re-encoded as JPEG; `dragon-fruit-1.jpg` and `dragon-fruit-3.jpg` were also cropped (to remove a printed pattern on a plate and a camera date stamp). Files are stored locally and are never hotlinked.

Attribution is required for CC BY and CC BY-SA files; CC0 and public domain files are credited voluntarily. Adaptations of CC BY-SA files (resize, crop) stay under the same license.

Demo/local-dev seed data only (see `.claude/rules/02-security-and-data.rule.md` §6).
